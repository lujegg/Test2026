Manual NodeList required far more lines of code, approximately 100 lines, whereas smart pointer only required 50, around half the amount. Additionally, we did not need
to utilize the rule of threes whenn dealing with smart pointer, whereas Manual Nodelist required all 3 just to prevent corrupting memory.
Additionally, when testing in Part C, these bugs are only possible on Manual NodeList, and would not have been possible when using smart pointers as it does not
utilize destructors, copy constructors, or assignment operators. Overall, smart pointer is less complex on the programmer, and needs to be manually programmed
in, whereas smart pointer does it automatically.
